# Welcome to My Lab Notes

  

Welcome to the blog section of my site — part lab journal, part knowledge base, and part creative outlet for all things cybersecurity and development.

  

This blog exists for a few key reasons:

  

## <div style="background:blue">1. To Document What I Learn</div>

  

Most of my time outside formal studies is spent researching, breaking, or building systems. Whether I’m testing buffer overflows, exploring attack chains in my home lab, building a Python/C log analyser, or diving into detection engineering — I like to write it all down.

  

This blog is where I turn messy lab notes into organized knowledge. If I can explain something clearly, I know I’ve truly learned it.

  

## 2. To Build in Public

  

This site — including the terminal interface — is a project in itself. It reflects the way I like to work: curious, iterative, and technically transparent. Everything from CTF walkthroughs to personal tooling will live here, organized by tags and topic.

  

Expect breakdowns of attack simulations, detection rules I’ve tested, mini write-ups on CVEs, security engineering experiments, and lessons learned from building out my own SOC lab.
